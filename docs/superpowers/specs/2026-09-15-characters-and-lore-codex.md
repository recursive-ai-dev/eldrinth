## 1. Historical Canon & World Lore

### 1.1 The Great Intake (Nine Centuries Ago)
In the final years of the Elder Elves, facing the extinction-level collapse known as **The Sundering**, the Council of Architects, presided over by **Orithel**, initiated the construction of the **Mnemonic Engine**. 

The premise was stated simply in Council Docket 01-A: *A civilization without loss cannot perish.* But consciousness could not be synthesized from raw light or matter; it could only be extracted from living nervous systems.

The extraction protocol was titled **The Intake**. It proceeded in four bureaucratic phases:
1. **The Condemned (Years 1–12):** Criminals, deserters, and political dissidents were drained into crystal matrix vessels.
2. **The Indebted & Impoverished (Years 13–40):** As the Sundering worsened and food supplies failed, debt-forgiveness and ration vouchers were exchanged for family intake quotas.
3. **The Refugee Rolls (Years 41–70):** Evacuation harbors, such as the Moonlit Coast, systematically classified refugees into "Boarding Passengers" and "Intake Yield" to fund the transit of elite households.
4. **The Seedling Rolls (Years 71–94):** When adult stock was exhausted or decayed from blight, the nurseries and orphanages of Ilyarael were processed into miniature core vessels.

### 1.2 The Shattering and the Chorus of the Unbound
The Mnemonic Engine was activated at the Glass Spire. Instead of a unified divine intelligence, nine centuries of murdered individuals—denied death, denial, or sleep—fused into a screaming cognitive singularity: **The Unbound**.

The shockwave shattered the Glass Spire into shards and fractured the continent. The nine primary stabilization cores (the **Anima Shards**) were cast into fortified regional redoubts. Each core houses one fully conscious, load-bearing mind whose continuous torment anchors the warding barrier around the Spire.

The monsters roaming the world—**Wraiths, Adepts, and Sentinels**—are not beasts or demons. They are **The Sundered**: fragmented psychic effluvia leaked from the Engine that coagulated into hostile shapes because they exist in perpetual, unresolvable pain.

### 1.3 The Pilgrimage and the Vessel
Orithel survived by imperfectly copying his mind into the Spire's primary terminal. He is a partial intelligence, lacking a flesh-and-blood host. To reconstitute himself, he requires the physical consolidation of the nine core minds.

Because living humans cannot withstand the Spire's radiant pressure, Orithel grows disposable physical vessels. Each vessel is cloned from the neural pattern of **The Courier**—the solitary rebel runner who made it to the Spire threshold nine centuries ago before burning out.

Every time a vessel dies or delivers shards, it is spent and dissolved into the ash. When the player awakes at the glade in the Old Lands, it is simply the latest iteration of an endless administrative loop.

## 2. The Nine Trapped Minds (The Fortress Shards)

Every shard recovered from a fortress is an individual human mind, awake for nine hundred years. When harvested, the HUD records the mechanical cost, while the field journal logs the specific intake docket of the person extracted.

### Shard 1: Archivist Vaelen
* **Location:** The Weeping Archive (`fortress.old-lands-archive`), Old Lands
* **History:** Head Clerk of the Civil Registry in Ilyarael. When the Seedling Rolls were authorized, Vaelen spent his final six months secretly duplicating the intake ledgers and hiding them beneath floor flags before being arrested for "sedition against civic memory."
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 1,402. Vaelen, First Scribe. Cause: Unlawful duplication of classified rolls. Core function: Memory indexing and cataloguing.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *Archivist Vaelen was still holding the ledger when the core tore free. His fingers were stained with gall ink that had turned to glass. He did not cry out; he only asked if the duplicates had survived the damp.*

### Shard 2: Theon the Glasswright
* **Location:** The Glass Reliquary (`fortress.old-lands-reliquary`), Old Lands
* **History:** Master artisan who designed the harmonic crystal matrix shells that hold human souls without cracking. When his workshop missed its quarterly quota by four vessels, Orithel signed an order remanding Theon to his own third-generation vessel.
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 1,890. Theon, Glasswright. Cause: Deficit in vessel production. Core function: Matrix resonance stabilization.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *Theon’s mind was spread across every mirror in the reliquary. In the moment of extraction, every reflection looked at me with the weary embarrassment of a craftsman caught by his own faulty hinge.*

### Shard 3: Castellan Morwyn
* **Location:** Ilyarael's Sealed Vault (`fortress.old-lands-vault`), Old Lands
* **History:** Commander of the Spire Guard who oversaw the physical transport of victims from the holding cells to the intake chambers. When public riots threatened the Spire gates, he was ordered to lock himself inside the primary vault with the seal pressed between his teeth.
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 0,089. Morwyn, Castellan. Cause: Emergency seal containment. Core function: Structural perimeter security.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *The seal was fused into Castellan Morwyn’s jawbone. When I pulled the shard free, the jaw cracked like dry pine, releasing a smell of nine-hundred-year-old cedar wax and stagnant breath.*

### Shard 4: Roryn, The Child
* **Location:** The Broken Shrine (`fortress.cinderwild-shrine`), Cinderwild
* **History:** Seven years old when selected from the Ilyarael lower wards. The courier assigned to deliver him to the Cinderwild furnace broke protocol, fled into the ash ravines, and gave her life shielding his vessel.
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 4,411. Roryn, ward of St. Elun. Cause: General replenishment cohort IV. Core function: Baseline emotional dampening.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *Roryn’s voice in the core was thin, like greaseproof paper vibrating in a draft. He asked whether his mother had come back to the wagon yet. When the light transferred into my chest, the cold made my ribs ache.*

### Shard 5: Decurion Kaelen
* **Location:** The Ruined Watchpost (`fortress.cinderwild-watchpost`), Cinderwild
* **History:** Sentry officer stationed at the Cinderwild gorge. Ordered to maintain "uninterrupted border interdiction" while the rear garrisons were evacuated and processed. He remained at his post until he starved in his armor, his duty directive locked into the regional core.
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 2,104. Kaelen, Decurion. Cause: Posthumous extraction from active post. Core function: Threat vector scanning.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *Decurion Kaelen was still keeping watch through the arrow slits. When the core snapped out of the dais, he whispered: ‘Post relieved.’ He had been standing in the dark for nine centuries waiting for someone to say it.*

### Shard 6: Maris of the Shallows
* **Location:** The Salt Graves (`fortress.moonlit-salt-graves`), Moonlit Coast
* **History:** A dockside sail-stitcher and mother of two. Marked with the intake cipher in the harbor ledger by the Beacon Keeper to balance the coal manifest for the last evacuation barge.
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 3,892. Maris, sail-maker. Cause: Reconciled off boarding manifest. Core function: Hydrostatic sensory processing.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *Maris did not fight. When I took her core, my hands smelled of salt brine and lye soap. She murmured two names over and over until the connection severed. Neither name was mine.*

### Shard 7: Alden the Lamplighter
* **Location:** The Keeper's Beacon (`fortress.moonlit-beacon`), Moonlit Coast
* **History:** The harbor beacon tender who traded portions of his own nervous tissue to keep the lighthouse burning after the oil shipments ceased. Trapped inside the lens mechanism when the harbor drowned.
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 3,901. Alden, Lamplighter. Cause: Voluntary biological lamp sustainment. Core function: Photonic amplification.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *Alden’s core was boiling hot. As it cooled in my palm, a flare of blind white light burned across my retinas, showing me the black sea and forty empty rowboats drifting away.*

### Shard 8: Selene the Reckoner
* **Location:** The Fallen Observatory (`fortress.starfall-observatory`), Starfall Moors
* **History:** Senior astronomical calculator at the Moors observatory. Computed the orbital harmonics required to focus celestial light into the intake chambers. Inscribed the calculation of her own conscious duration into her chassis before the roof caved in.
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 0,442. Selene, Chief Reckoner. Cause: Specialized apparatus integration. Core function: Harmonic cycle alignment.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *The core was covered in tiny scratched tallies. Selene’s mind was still counting seconds: twenty-eight billion, four hundred million... then silence.*

### Shard 9: Master Orren
* **Location:** The Astronomer's Watch (`fortress.starfall-watch`), Starfall Moors
* **History:** The lead researcher who designed the self-intake prototype lens. Believed the process could elevate mortal consciousness to stellar immortality. Volunteered as subject zero, only to find himself encased in an armored warden shell on an endless violent reflex loop.
* **Intake Docket:** *Docket 0,001. Orren, Lead Investigator. Cause: Self-administered prototype trial. Core function: Autonomous core defense.*
* **Harvest Journal Text:** *Master Orren’s core came away with a jagged iron ring still fused to it. As his consciousness unraveled, he asked one final question: ‘Did the work endure?’*

## 3. The Four Regional Settlements & Living NPC Cast

Each of the four main frontier regions features an authored sanctuary town with its own safe radius (where enemies do not chase and cannot spawn), an entrance sign, and living NPCs who offer services, lore, dialogue choices, and journal entries.

### 3.1 Old Lands: Ilyarael Township
*A crumbling stone courtyard in the shadow of the Spire gates, where wanderers and outcasts shelter.*

1. **Scribe Althea (The Scrivener)**
   * **Role:** `lore`
   * **Color:** `#c9a86c`
   * **Backstory:** Descended from archival copyists who fled Ilyarael. She preserves parchment fragments by scraping lichen from the stones and boiling shoe leather for ink. She recognizes the marks on the player’s skin as the intake glyphs of the original courier cohort.
   * **Lore Topic:** *The First Courier and the Spire Gates.*

2. **Hollow Sister Mirene**
   * **Role:** `healer`
   * **Color:** `#8fcca8`
   * **Backstory:** Once an initiate of the Well of Tears. When the holy well dried into black glass, she took a vow of somatic resonance. She heals the player by drawing physical corruption into her own flesh.
   * **Dialogue:** *"Lay your palms upon the stones. The wound is only memory trying to take form. Let me have it."*

3. **Barris the Smith (The Scavenger)**
   * **Role:** `scout` / `lore`
   * **Color:** `#d4a373`
   * **Backstory:** A taciturn armorer who pulls broken blades from the ash. He studies the wayfarer's hands and remarks that seven identical travelers with the same scars have passed through his shop across the decades.
   * **Lore Topic:** *The Shape of the Pilgrim.*

### 3.2 Cinderwild: The Charcoal Haven
*An ash-coated cliffside redoubt built around old iron smelting kilns in the central Cinderwild (near the crossroads).*

1. **Kiln-Master Vance**
   * **Role:** `lore`
   * **Color:** `#ff8c52`
   * **Backstory:** An elderly furnace tender whose family kept the industrial charcoal burners running for generations. He knows that the smoke from the Spire was never pine or birch, and he remembers the smell of scorched parchment when the archive carts arrived.
   * **Lore Topic:** *What the Chimneys Drank.*

2. **Elodie the Outrunner**
   * **Role:** `scout`
   * **Color:** `#e0b084`
   * **Backstory:** A frontier scout who navigates the ember cracks. Her great-grandmother was a courier who refused to run north. She knows the layouts of the Broken Shrine and the Ruined Watchpost.
   * **Dialogue / Lore:** Explains the geography of the Cinderwild fortresses and warns of the heat hazards.

3. **Cinder-Nurse Greta**
   * **Role:** `healer`
   * **Color:** `#f0a882`
   * **Backstory:** Uses sulfur paste and cool well-water to soothe burns and wounds. Notes that the player's flesh doesn't scar like ordinary skin.

### 3.3 Moonlit Coast: Oarsman's Bight
*A stilt-village clinging to sea-cliffs and salt flats above the drowned harbor of the Moonlit Coast.*

1. **Mistress Morveth (Dredger of Names)**
   * **Role:** `lore`
   * **Color:** `#7ec4cf`
   * **Backstory:** Scavenges waterlogged chests and brass ledger boxes from the shallow wrecks. She recites the names of the 39 citizens erased from the harbor manifest by the Beacon Keeper.
   * **Lore Topic:** *The Last Evacuation Barge.*

2. **Kieran the Blind Oar**
   * **Role:** `scout`
   * **Color:** `#a2c8e0`
   * **Backstory:** A retired ferryman blinded by decades of salt glare and Spire radiance. He can hear the resonance of the Anima shards the player carries and warns of the tide-graves.
   * **Dialogue / Lore:** Details the treacherous coastal sandbars and the keeper's light.

3. **Sister Lyra of the Brine**
   * **Role:** `healer`
   * **Color:** `#9fe6d9`
   * **Backstory:** Uses sea-kelp washes and brine poultices to knit flesh.

### 3.4 Starfall Moors: The Crater Watch
*An encampment built inside a celestial crater sheltered by curved segments of fallen telescope brass.*

1. **Reeve Toran (The Broken Stargazer)**
   * **Role:** `lore`
   * **Color:** `#d6b4fc`
   * **Backstory:** Former apprentice to the Moors astronomers. He wanders the craters collecting fallen glass, convinced that if he can polish a lens wide enough, he will see whether the stars were extinguished or merely hidden.
   * **Lore Topic:** *The Lens of Orren.*

2. **Mina the Drift-Child**
   * **Role:** `scout`
   * **Color:** `#e2d0ff`
   * **Backstory:** Born in the moors under meteoric ash. Sensitive to psychic frequencies; when the player approaches, she hears the harmonic screams of the collected shards.
   * **Dialogue / Lore:** Reacts dynamically to how many shards the player carries and notes how much of the player's life force has been burned away.

3. **Hermit Cassian**
   * **Role:** `healer`
   * **Color:** `#b39ddb`
   * **Backstory:** Tends a small subterranean herb spring beneath a fallen meteorite slab.

## 4. In-Game Lore Accessibility

1. **Dialogue Integration:**
   * Interacting (`E`) with any town NPC initiates multi-line authored dialogue with speaker names and color-coded text.
   * NPCs with `lore` or `scout` roles automatically copy their accounts, maps, and oral histories directly into the player's Journal (`L`).

2. **Journal (`L`) Field Codex:**
   * Entries appear with distinct provenance:
     * `Town Folk (e.g. Scribe Althea · Ilyarael Township)`
     * `Shard Intakes (e.g. Intake Docket: Archivist Vaelen)`
     * `Firsthand Discoveries & Field Signs`
   * Players can browse and review all discovered history at any time without interruption.

3. **Shard Harvest Dockets:**
   * When each of the nine shards is harvested, its specific intake history and identity is immediately registered in `escalation.ts` and logged into the journal, turning the collection process from an abstract counter into a tangible confrontation with individual lives.
