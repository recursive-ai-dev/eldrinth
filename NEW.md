# Eldrinth Graphical Overhaul Plan (`shed-dev-sprites`)

Development game plan for overhauling Eldrinth's visual assets and rendering pipeline using the updated `~/Projects/shed-dev-sprites` generator (shipped in **v1.4.0 — The Living Frontier**).

## Current State

- **World atlases (`src/assets/sprites/`):** Generated by `tools/spritegen/gen_sprites.py` and `tools/spritegen/catalog.py` (88 atlases, 3,524 verified frame hashes) from the synced `shed-dev-sprites` v3.0 generator.
- **Equipment, field kit & regional scenes (`src/assets/equipment/`, `src/assets/field-kit/`, `src/assets/scenes/`):** Generated from `~/Projects/shed-dev-sprites` via `gen_equipment_icons.py`, `gen_field_kit.py`, and `gen_scenes.py` with manifest, SHA-256 checksums, and provenance verification.
- **Capabilities integrated from `~/Projects/shed-dev-sprites`:**
  - Upgraded `human.py` and `creature.py` with monotonic pale-to-deep skin ramps, gentler face shading, and full multi-clip animations (`idle`, `walk`, `attack`, `cast`, `hurt`, `death`).
  - 47-tile Blob Wang autotiling (`BLOB_47_MASKS` in `generator/math/wang.py`), upgraded fBm/noise, and CIELAB master-palette locking (`generator/math/palette.py`).
  - Upgraded `settlement.py` plus `war.py` (`structure` for halls, smithies, watchtowers, shrines, barracks, and mills).
  - `scene.py` and `font.py` for 192×108 regional frontier arrival banners and Atlas landscape illustrations.

## Phase 2 — Characters, Enemies & Animation States

- [x] Expand `tools/spritegen/catalog.py` beyond `walk`/`attack` to generate `idle`, `cast` (for adepts, wardens, and boss), `hurt`, and `death` clips.
- [x] Wire the new animation states into `src/game/render/spriteAtlas.ts` and `src/game/render/renderer.ts`.
- [x] Give regional elite sentinels and fortress wardens distinct silhouettes and palettes (`enemy.warden.old-lands`, `enemy.warden.cinderwild`, `enemy.warden.moonlit-coast`, `enemy.warden.starfall-moors`).
- [x] Regenerate all 13 canonical lore NPCs with the updated skin/face shading and dedicated `idle` stance frames.

## Phase 3 — Terrain, Autotiling & Palette Cohesion

- [x] Upgrade core terrain sets (`tile.grass`, `tile.path`, `tile.water`, `tile.rwall`, `tile.sand`, plus regional variants) from 16-tile Wang to 47-tile Blob Wang autotiling for clean inner/outer corners, shorelines, and joined masonry parapets.
- [x] Update tile bitmask lookup in `src/game/render/renderer.ts` and `src/game/render/spriteAtlas.ts` (`BLOB_47_MASKS`, `canonicalBlobMask`, `blobMaskIndex`) to support 47-tile Blob masks.
- [x] Lock each of the four regions (Old Lands, Cinderwild, Moonlit Coast, Starfall Moors) to a cohesive CIELAB master palette ramp (`REGIONAL_MASTER_PALETTES`) with dedicated regional terrain and foliage sets.

## Phase 4 — Architecture, Fortresses & Landmarks

- [x] Regenerate the four regional house styles (`prop.house.oldstone`, `prop.house.cinder`, `prop.house.coastal`, `prop.house.moor`) using the upgraded `settlement.py` with centered doorway joinery.
